Frances Haugen's nonprofit

Social media can serve the common good.

Beyond the Screen brings technical expertise and insider experience to the growing ecosystem of checks and balances needed to make social platforms safer, more transparent, and more accountable.

Get involved Previous projects

Social platforms shape childhood, democracy, public health, and the information environment. The people affected by those systems deserve enough visibility to understand what is happening and enough leverage to change it.

Previous projects

Foundational work from Beyond the Screen

A standard of care

A practical map of platform harms and the product choices that can prevent or reduce them, giving policymakers, investors, researchers, and civil society a shared basis for action.

Minimum viable queries

A data-access standard for the questions the public and independent researchers must be able to ask in order to evaluate platform risks and progress.

Simulated social network

A tool for testing how design choices influence online dynamics, helping people see how platform harms emerge and how different interventions can change outcomes.

Why now

The public cannot govern systems it cannot inspect.

For years, platforms have controlled the data, definitions, and narratives around their own harms. That information imbalance keeps experts, regulators, parents, investors, and communities from making grounded decisions.

Beyond the Screen focuses on the infrastructure for accountability: shared technical context, credible data access, and concrete safety expectations that make better platforms possible.
